# Warranty and Product Protection Policy

Document ID: POL-WAR-007
Owner: Product Services
Last reviewed: January 2026

## Manufacturer warranty

Every new product carries the manufacturer's own warranty. The length varies
by category and by brand. Typical coverage:

- Small kitchen appliances: 1 year
- Major appliances: 1 year parts and labour, 10 years on sealed components
- Televisions: 1 year
- Laptops and tablets: 1 year
- Power tools: 3 years
- Mattresses: 10 years, prorated after year 3

The manufacturer warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship. It does
**not** cover accidental damage, liquid damage, cosmetic wear, or damage from
misuse.

> Note: a warranty claim is different from a return. Returns are governed by
> POL-RET-004 and are time-limited to the return window. Warranty claims can
> be made after the return window has closed.

## Extended protection plans

Extended protection plans may be purchased within **30 days** of the original
purchase date. Pricing depends on the item value.

| Item price       | 2-year plan | 3-year plan |
| ---------------- | ----------- | ----------- |
| $50 to $199      | $9.99       | $14.99      |
| $200 to $499     | $24.99      | $39.99      |
| $500 to $999     | $49.99      | $79.99      |
| $1,000 and above | $99.99      | $149.99     |

Extended plans begin the day the manufacturer warranty expires, except for
accidental damage cover, which starts on day one.

## What the extended plan adds

- Accidental damage from handling, including drops and spills
- Power surge protection
- Normal wear and tear failures
- One battery replacement for laptops and phones, if capacity drops below 50%
- No-lemon guarantee: after **three** qualifying repairs, the item is replaced

## Filing a claim

Claims can be filed online or by phone. The customer needs the original
receipt and the product serial number.

Standard claim handling:

1. Claim submitted and reviewed, normally within 2 business days
2. If approved, a prepaid shipping label is issued for depot repair
3. Repair is completed within 10 business days of receipt
4. If the item cannot be repaired economically, a replacement or a store
   credit at the original purchase price is issued

For major appliances and televisions over 55 inches, an in-home technician
visit is scheduled instead of depot repair. In-home visits are typically
available within 5 business days.

## Exclusions

Protection plans do not cover:

- Loss or theft
- Intentional damage
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect function
- Consumable parts such as filters, bulbs, and batteries beyond the single
  covered replacement
- Commercial or rental use of a consumer-grade product
- Damage caused by unauthorised third-party repair

## Cancelling a plan

A protection plan can be cancelled within **30 days** for a full refund if no
claim has been filed. After 30 days, a prorated refund is issued based on the
remaining term, less any claims paid.
